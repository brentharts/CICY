\begin{abstract}
The Spectre-substrate proposal for the Penrose conformal crossover surface makes
two data-facing statements: a parity-asymmetric crossover
(Conjecture~1), and a log-periodic modulation of primordial
statistics with the parameter-free period
$\log\lam^{2}=\log(4+\sqrt{15})=\CFLogPeriod$ in $\ln k$. This paper
confronts both with current data. On parity: the sky is currently
measured to be parity-odd, with the 2026 joint ACT~DR6 $+$ Planck~PR4
birefringence analysis giving $\beta=\CFBetaMeasured^{\circ}\pm
\CFBetaSigma^{\circ}$ ($\CFBetaZFace\sigma$ at face-value instrument
priors, $\CFBetaZDust\sigma$ under conservative dust modeling); the
disfavoring branch of the conjecture is not triggered. The substrate's
one intrinsic chiral order parameter, the exact splitting maximum
$10g/(8+10g)$ with $g=4-\sqrt{15}$, yields under a stated unit postulate
the sharp candidate $\beta_{\rm pred}=70/67-(40/201)\sqrt{15}=
\CFBetaPred^{\circ}$, which sits $\CFBetaPull\sigma$ from the
measurement --- recorded as a falsifiable speculation, not a claim. On
log-periodicity: the first direct search at the substrate frequency is
performed by generalized least squares on $\CFNBinsSPT$ SPT-3G~2018,
$\CFNBinsACT$ ACT~DR6, and $\CFNBinsPlanck$ Planck plik-lite band
powers, with templates projected through full CAMB transfer functions
and smooth directions marginalized. All three datasets return null; the
joint bound is $|A|<\CFULTri$ at 95\%, with the second discrete-scale-
invariance harmonic bounded at $\CFULHarmTwo$. A calibration-era table
states what decides the parity question instrumentally: at
$0.05^{\circ}$ absolute calibration the candidate is tested at
$\CFDetectSO\sigma$; at the $0.01^{\circ}$ class it is separated from
the WMAP$+$Planck value at $\CFDiscLB\sigma$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

This paper is supplementary material to
\emph{"The Spectre Substrate: A Chiral Monotile Vacuum for the Penrose Conformal Crossover Surface"},
and its Conjecture~1 (page~1 Section~\emph{The crossover screen and the two monotile phases}).
Conjecture~1 predicts a parity-asymmetric crossover: primordial chiral gravitational waves or
parity-odd CMB (cosmic microwave background radiation) correlations of primordial origin are consistent with it,
and exact parity-evenness to observational limits disfavors it. 
The falsifiability section (page~9) predicts a log-periodic modulation of primordial statistics at the substrate's fundamental unit: period
$\log\lam^{2}=\log(4+\sqrt{15})$ in $\ln k$, frequency
$\omega_{*}=2\pi/\log\lam^{2}=\CFOmegaStar$ per $e$-fold of scale, with
\emph{no free parameter in the frequency}.

Neither statement had been given numbers. This paper gives both numbers,
in the discipline of the series \cite{SuppMath,SuppStrings,SuppTwistor, SuppNariai, SuppMonotile}: 
every figure quoted here is written by
\texttt{paper/make\_crossover\_facts.py}, which runs the analysis module
\texttt{pyCICY.theories.parity} end to end, so the prose and the
computation cannot drift apart. \Cref{sec:parity} collects the
birefringence measurements and renders the verdict the data currently
support. \Cref{sec:candidate} records the substrate's quantitative
candidate for $\beta$ --- speculation-grade, exactly stated, and sitting
startlingly close to the measurement. \Cref{sec:transfer} makes the
substrate$\to$$P(k)$ transfer assumption explicit and derives the search
template from discrete scale invariance rather than positing it.
\Cref{sec:search} performs the search on real band powers ---
SPT-3G~2018, ACT~DR6, and the Planck plik-lite spectra, whose
$\ell\ge30$ reach adds slightly over one full modulation period ---
and sets the joint bound. \Cref{sec:calibration} tabulates what the
coming calibration era decides. \Cref{sec:boundary} restates the
series boundary for a data paper, where the categories acquire an
observational flavor: what is measured, what is bounded, what is
predicted, and what does not yet exist as an observable.

\section{The parity channel: what is measured}
\label{sec:parity}

Isotropic cosmic birefringence --- a uniform rotation $\beta$ of the
CMB linear polarization between emission and observation --- is the
cleanest parity-odd observable in cosmology: it generates $EB$ and $TB$
correlations through the exact mixing
$C_{\ell}^{EB}=\tfrac12\sin(4\beta)\,(C_{\ell}^{EE}-C_{\ell}^{BB})$,
$C_{\ell}^{TB}=\sin(2\beta)\,C_{\ell}^{TE}$, identities the analysis
module implements and verifies as identities (rotation invariance of
$EE+BB$ and of $TE^{2}+TB^{2}$, round-trip exactness, all at
$10^{-15}$). The measurement chain, all by the foreground-$EB$
(Minami--Komatsu) method or with instrument-angle priors:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
dataset & $\beta$ [deg] & ref.\\
\midrule
Planck PR3 & $0.35\pm0.14$ & \cite{MinamiKomatsu2020}\\
Planck PR4 & $0.30\pm0.11$ & \cite{DiegoPalazuelos2022}\\
WMAP9 $+$ PR4 & $0.342^{+0.094}_{-0.091}$ & \cite{EskiltKomatsu2022}\\
Cosmoglobe DR1 & $0.26\pm0.10$ & \cite{Cosmoglobe2023}\\
ACT DR6 (as $\langle\psi\rangle$) & $0.20\pm0.08$ & \cite{ACTDR6}\\
ACT DR6 (cosmological) & $0.215\pm0.074$ & \cite{DPK2025}\\
\textbf{ACT DR6 $+$ PR4 joint} & $\mathbf{0.277\pm0.057}$ &
\cite{Eskilt2026}\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The headline is the 2026 joint analysis \cite{Eskilt2026}:
$\beta=\CFBetaMeasured^{\circ}\pm\CFBetaSigma^{\circ}$, nonzero at
$\CFBetaZFace\sigma$ taking the reported instrument priors at face
value, and still $\CFBetaZDust\sigma$ under a dust-$EB$ modeling
variant designed to be conservative. Three caveats are part of the
result, not footnotes to it: the entries share two systematics
(Galactic dust $EB$ modeling and absolute polarization-angle
calibration) and are correlated through reused Planck data; isotropic
birefringence is parity violation in photon \emph{propagation} --- a
Chern--Simons coupling to an axion-like field would produce it ---
so its detection is consistency with a parity-asymmetric crossover,
not identification of one; and Conjecture~1 as published is
qualitative.

The verdict, stated as the conjecture's own dichotomy requires: the
sky is currently measured to be parity-\emph{odd}. Exact
parity-evenness to observational limits --- the disfavoring branch ---
is not what the data show. The consistent branch is active, at
$3.5$--$4.9\sigma$ depending on how much of the systematic budget one
concedes.

\begin{remark}[The tensor channel does not yet exist]
The other parity observable named by Conjecture~1, the chirality of the
primordial gravitational-wave background, is not an observable of any
existing dataset: no such background has been detected
($r_{0.05}<\CFRUpper$ at 95\% \cite{BK18}), and the handedness of an
undetected signal is not a measurement. The analysis module encodes
this as a refusal (\texttt{NoSuchTheory}), in the discipline of
\cite{SuppStrings}: the channel opens if and when $r$ is detected. The
channel is nonetheless \emph{wired}: the BK18 archive \cite{BK18} ---
$\CFBKNSpectra$ cross-spectra in $\CFBKNBins$ band powers with
published window functions --- is parsed by the analysis module, and
projecting a tensor background at the current limit through the real
$150\,$GHz windows against the real uncertainties yields a total
signal-to-noise of $\CFBKChiralSN$: even a background saturating the
bound registers at barely more than $1\sigma$ in this spectrum alone,
which is the quantitative content of ``closed.''
\end{remark}

\section{The substrate's candidate for $\beta$}
\label{sec:candidate}

Conjecture~1 gains teeth only if the substrate names a number. It has
exactly one intrinsic chiral order parameter with a preferred magnitude:
the geometric splitting maximum of the Spectre$\to$Hat deformation
sweep, computed exactly in the companion paper (page~6, the area-form, Fact7) as

\begin{equation}
A_{\max}\;=\;\frac{10\,g}{8+10\,g}
\;=\;\frac{35}{67}-\frac{20}{201}\sqrt{15}
\;=\;0.1370166\ldots,
\qquad g=4-\sqrt{15}.
\label{eq:amax}
\end{equation}
That number was derived for the deformation analysis of the substrate
paper, before any comparison with polarization data was contemplated;
its reuse here is a reuse, not a fit.

\begin{spec}[The birefringence candidate]
\label{spec:beta}
Identify the substrate order parameter \cref{eq:amax} with the
Stokes-level chiral asymmetry of the crossover, and read the resulting
angle in degrees:
\begin{equation}
\beta_{\rm pred}
\;=\;2\,A_{\max}\ \mathrm{[deg]}
\;=\;\frac{70}{67}-\frac{40}{201}\sqrt{15}\ \mathrm{deg}
\;=\;0.2740332\ldots^{\circ}.
\label{eq:betapred}
\end{equation}
The factor $2$ is the spin-2 convention (the polarization plane rotates
by $\beta$ while the Stokes vector rotates by $2\beta$); the unit
postulate --- a dimensionless order parameter read in degrees --- is
the entire modeling step, and it is unjustified. Against the joint
measurement, $\beta_{\rm pred}$ sits at $\CFBetaPull\sigma$.
\end{spec}

Everything about \cref{spec:beta} is stated so that it can die. The
exact value \cref{eq:betapred} has no adjustable content; the
identification is either a coincidence among small numbers or a
prediction; and \cref{sec:calibration} says exactly when the
instruments will decide. 
It is recorded behind the speculation firewall of the source paper's
stratification for precisely this reason: a $0.05\sigma$ agreement
produced by a unit postulate is a target painted for future
calibration, not evidence.

\section{The transfer assumption, made explicit}
\label{sec:transfer}

The layer-two search is often phrased as ``look for the log-period.''
The honest version derives the template. If the crossover statistics
inherit the substitution's discrete scale invariance --- physics
unchanged under $k\to\lam^{2}k$ --- then any imprint on the primordial
spectrum is a periodic function of $\ln k$ with period $\log\lam^{2}$:
\begin{equation}
P(k)\;=\;P_{0}(k)\,
F\!\left(\ln k \bmod \log\lam^{2}\right),
\qquad
F(x)=1+\sum_{m\ge1}\bigl[a_{m}\cos(m\,\omega_{*}x)
+b_{m}\sin(m\,\omega_{*}x)\bigr],
\label{eq:dsi}
\end{equation}
and the searched amplitude is the leading Fourier mode of $F$. The
modeling ladder, in decreasing order of necessity: (i) discrete scale
invariance itself --- this is the content of the substrate prediction,
and it fixes the frequency and nothing else; (ii) multiplicative
imprint on $P(k)$, the minimal coupling of substrate modulation to
curvature perturbations; (iii) truncation at $m=1$, which is testable,
because the $m=2$ harmonic sits at $2\omega_{*}$ and the same machinery
bounds it (\cref{sec:search}). What discrete scale invariance does not
fix: the amplitude, the phase, and whether $F$ multiplies the spectrum
or its logarithm (indistinguishable at first order). One period of
\cref{eq:dsi} spans a factor $e^{\log\lam^{2}}=\CFPeriodFactor$ in $k$:
the modulation is slow, roughly one cycle per data octave-triple, which
is why the fit must marginalize smooth directions explicitly or the
signal hides in the tilt.

\section{The search on real band powers}
\label{sec:search}

The method is generalized least squares in band-power space. CAMB
projects the modulated primordial spectrum \cref{eq:dsi} (at unit
$a_{1}$, both quadratures) through the full transfer functions --- no
$k\simeq\ell/D$ shortcut --- and the resulting $\Delta D_{\ell}$
templates are binned through each dataset's own machinery: the candl
library \cite{candl} with its window functions and data models for
SPT-3G~2018 TT/TE/EE \cite{SPT3G} and ACT~DR6 TT/TE/EE \cite{ACTDR6},
and the plik-lite band powers, covariance and bin weights of Planck~2018
\cite{PlanckLikelihood,PlanckLitePy} for the third dataset. The design
matrix carries the two modulation quadratures and three smooth nuisance
directions (overall amplitude, the tilt derivative $dD_{\ell}/dn_{s}$
computed by finite difference, and a temperature-vs-polarization
calibration split); the noise is each likelihood's full band-power
covariance; the fiducial cosmology is Planck~2018 and is \emph{not}
refit per dataset --- a stated limitation, mitigated by the
marginalized smooth directions, and visible as an acceptable fiducial
$\chi^{2}$ of $\CFChiFidPlanck$ on $\CFNBinsPlanck$ Planck bins.

\begin{prop}[The layer-two bound]
\label{prop:bound}
At the substrate frequency $\omega_{*}=\CFOmegaStar$ per $\ln k$, the
leading log-periodic modulation amplitude of the primordial spectrum is
\begin{equation}
|A| \;<\; \CFULTri
\qquad(95\%,\ \text{SPT-3G}+\text{ACT DR6}+\text{Planck plik-lite}),
\end{equation}
with all three datasets individually consistent with zero
($\Delta\chi^{2}$ of $\CFDChiSPT$, $\CFDChiACT$, $\CFDChiPlanck$ on two
degrees of freedom) and the SPT and ACT quadrature phases mutually
inconsistent --- the two ground experiments prefer orthogonal phases,
which is what noise does. The second discrete-scale-invariance harmonic
is bounded independently: $|a_{2}|<\CFULHarmTwo$ at $2\omega_{*}$
($\Delta\chi^{2}=\CFDChiHarmTwo$).
\end{prop}

Two robustness statements accompany \cref{prop:bound}. First, the
stated fiducial limitation is demonstrated harmless rather than argued
away: adding finite-difference derivative templates in $H_{0}$,
$\omega_{b}$ and $\omega_{c}$ to the design matrix --- a
six-dimensional smooth marginalization --- moves the joint bound from
$\CFULTri$ to $\CFULTriRobust$, a shift of two percent of itself.
Second, the $BB$ channel is searched and found powerless at current
sensitivity: the SPTpol $BB$ band powers ($\CFNBinsBB$ bins) admit
$|A|<\CFULBB$, two orders of magnitude weaker than the primary
channels, because the lensing kernel washes the modulation out of $BB$;
it is reported so that the channel's silence is a number rather than an
omission.

Planck's contribution is decisive and is exactly the two extra octaves:
its $\ell\ge30$ reach adds slightly over one full modulation period
below the ground experiments' bands, and its single-dataset limit
($\CFULPlanck$) is already twice as strong as the SPT$+$ACT
combination ($\CFULDuo$). A frequency scan confirms the machinery
responds sensibly away from $\omega_{*}$ --- limits tighten
monotonically with frequency as tilt degeneracy releases --- with no
anomaly anywhere.

The prediction survives as a constraint. The substrate paper's layer
two said ``a log-periodic modulation \ldots at sufficient sensitivity'';
this section replaces the ellipsis: current sensitivity is
$\sigma_{A}\approx\CFSigAmpTri$ at the predicted frequency, the data
are null there, and any future claim of the substrate's imprint on
$P(k)$ must live below $\CFULTri$.

\section{A proposed percent-level foreground, priced}
\label{sec:etg}

Gjergo and Kroupa have proposed that the dust-thermalized light of massive early-type galaxies (ETG) forming at
$z\sim15$--$20$ constitutes an overlooked CMB foreground, accounting
for ``1.4\% up to the full present-day CMB energy density''
\cite{GjergoKroupa}. Both channels of this paper live in CMB data, so
the claim sits underneath our verdicts and must be priced, not cited.
The analysis module reproduces their pipeline first --- the downsizing
timescales, the energy densities and their $1.4\%$ ratio, and the
dust-temperature consistency ($L=10^{15}L_{\odot}$ through
$10\,$kpc gives $49\,$K, redshifting to $\sim2.7\,$K) all check ---
with one finding in the two-route spirit: their Eq.~(5) as printed,
$15\,{\rm Mpc}/800\,{\rm kpc}-1$, evaluates to $\CFGKZfComputed$,
not the quoted $\CFGKZfPrinted$; the conclusion it feeds, a formation
epoch inside the 21-cm window $15<z<20$, holds either way.

The proposal then meets two walls its authors defer to future work, one
per spectral variant. If the component is a \emph{graybody} --- dust
emissivity $\propto\nu^{\beta_{d}}$ with $\beta_{d}>0$ --- the
power-law factor survives redshift, the component is not a blackbody
today, and the FIRAS monopole bounds its energy fraction directly:
profiling out the one exact degeneracy (a CMB temperature shift), the
admissible fraction is $f<\CFGKFirasBest$--$\CFGKFirasWorst$ across
$\beta_{d}\in[0.5,2]$, a factor $7$--$50$ below the conservative
claim. If instead the component is \emph{spectrally exact} ---
optically thick emission indistinguishable from the CMB monopole, the
authors' own consistency argument --- the spectrum is silent but the
sources are not: the field consists of $4.4\times10^{7}$ discrete
objects, $\CFGKPerBeam$ per Planck beam by their own count, and its
Poisson shot noise is an anisotropy floor. In the maximally
conservative configuration (equal luminosities, no clustering, both of
which can only raise the power), a fraction $f=0.014$ predicts
$D_{\ell=3000}^{\rm shot}$ exceeding the \emph{total} measured
$150\,$GHz sky power --- CMB plus every foreground, no component
separation assumed --- by a factor $\CFGKOverpred$; the requirement
of not outshining everything measured gives $f<\CFGKShotFmax$, a
factor $\CFGKClaimedOverAllowed$ below the claim.

The impact on this paper's verdicts is then arithmetic. Appending an
ETG shot-noise template to the smooth-direction block of the
layer-two fit moves the joint bound from $\CFULTri$ to
$\CFULTriShot$; at the spectrally and statistically admissible
$f\lesssim3\times10^{-5}$, the perturbation to the $EB$-derived
birefringence angle and to every band-power fit is
$\mathcal{O}(f)$, three orders of magnitude below the quoted
uncertainties. The sigma ratings of \cref{sec:parity,sec:search}
stand unchanged --- with the foreground now priced rather than
ignored, which is what this series means by taking a proposal seriously.

\section{The calibration era}
\label{sec:calibration}

The parity question is calibration-limited: the joint $\beta$
measurement leans on instrument-angle priors, and its
$\CFBetaZFace\sigma$ face value relaxes to $\CFBetaZDust\sigma$ under
conservative dust modeling. What removes the priors from the argument
is hardware --- absolute polarization-angle calibration. The
arithmetic of the coming era, with $\beta_{\rm pred}$ from
\cref{spec:beta}:

violation, it discriminates between candidate values of it. The
program goals invoked are the published calibration science cases of
Simons Observatory and LiteBIRD \cite{LiteBIRDBiref}; the table is
arithmetic, not forecasted likelihoods, and inherits their timelines.

\section{The boundary, restated for data}
\label{sec:boundary}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \emph{Measured.} The sky's parity: odd, at
$\CFBetaZDust$--$\CFBetaZFace\sigma$ depending on the systematic
concession. Conjecture~1's disfavoring branch is not triggered.
\item \emph{Bounded.} The substrate's layer-two imprint:
$|A|<\CFULTri$ at the parameter-free frequency, $|a_{2}|<\CFULHarmTwo$
at its second harmonic. A null with a number is worth more than a
prediction without one.
\item \emph{Predicted, and honestly graded.} $\beta_{\rm pred}=
\CFBetaPred^{\circ}$, exact in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{15})$, speculation-%
grade because of one stated unit postulate, currently at
$\CFBetaPull\sigma$ --- and scheduled for judgment by hardware.
\item \emph{Does not yet exist.} The chirality of the tensor
background, pending a detection of the background itself; and a
\emph{derived} amplitude for the layer-two modulation --- discrete
scale invariance fixes the frequency and nothing else, and pretending
otherwise would be a free parameter wearing a costume.
\item \emph{Stated limitations.} The fiducial cosmology is fixed at
Planck 2018 rather than refit jointly (protected by marginalized smooth
directions; a full joint fit is the obvious upgrade); the plik-lite
$EE$ data enter with their published foreground marginalization; and
the ground-experiment nuisance treatment uses each likelihood's default
prior centers. None of these plausibly manufactures or hides a
percent-level log-periodic signal, but all three belong in this list.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The parity conjecture survives its first confrontation in the strongest way available to a
qualitative claim: the sky is measured to be parity-odd \cite{Shamir2025},
such as specific configurations of the CMB bispectrum or B-mode polarization patterns \cite{Eskilt2026, Kamionkowski2010, BenDavid2011, Shiraishi2014, Murai2023},
and the Spectre substrate's one intrinsic chiral
number lands within a twentieth of a standard deviation of the measured
angle under a single stated postulate. The log-periodic prediction
survives in the way quantitative claims survive: bounded, at the
percent level, at a frequency it named in advance.
